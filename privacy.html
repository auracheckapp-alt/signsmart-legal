Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

The short version: when you scan a dealer quote, it's sent to Anthropic's Claude AI to be read and explained. Our server doesn't keep your quote. Anthropic, which provides the AI, automatically deletes it within 30 days and doesn't use it to train its models. There are no accounts. Your saved deals, state and settings are stored on your phone.

Who we are

SignSmart ("we", "us") is a car deal checker app operated by Austin Nieves. Contact us at auracheck.app@gmail.com.

Quotes you scan

When you scan a quote (a photo, a PDF or pasted text), the app shrinks any photo on your phone and sends the quote over an encrypted connection to our server, which passes it to Anthropic's Claude AI. Along with the quote, each scan sends the state you picked and your answer to "Is this your first car?", so the explanations can use them. Claude reads the line items and writes the explanations and suggested messages you see. Before your first scan, the app tells you this and asks you to continue.

  • We don't store or log your quotes. Our server handles the quote only for the time it takes to process it and keeps no copy.
  • Anthropic, which provides the AI, processes the quote on our behalf. It automatically deletes it within 30 days and doesn't use it to train its models. See Anthropic's data retention policy.
  • A quote can include personal details such as your name, address, phone number or the car's VIN. To limit what you share, you can crop or cover those parts before scanning, or paste just the price lines.

Fair-use limits

The app creates one random ID on your phone and stores it in the iPhone Keychain. It isn't linked to your name, email or Apple ID. The app sends it with each scan so we can apply fair-use limits. Our server, hosted by Cloudflare, keeps a count of scans per ID, counted per step (reading the quote and writing the explanations), with the time of each step, and nothing else: no quote content, name or contact details. We don't keep request logs. Like any website, Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver the request. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

The same random ID is used for purchases (see below). Because it's stored in the Keychain, it stays on your iPhone even after you use "Delete all my data" or delete and reinstall the app.

What stays on your phone

Your state, your onboarding answers, deals you save and your settings are stored only on your device. We don't keep them on our server. Your state and first-car answer are sent with each scan, as described above, and aren't stored. The Rate Check runs entirely on your phone; the credit range and rate you enter aren't sent anywhere.

Camera and photos

SignSmart asks for camera or photo library access only when you choose to scan a photo. It only uses the image you pick.

No accounts

You don't sign up or log in. We don't ask for your name, email address or phone number.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to manage the Deal Pass. The app gives RevenueCat the same random ID it uses for scan counts, and RevenueCat receives your purchase status from the App Store, along with basic technical information (such as device model, operating system version, app version, country and IP address) so it can confirm purchases and unlock paid features. RevenueCat does not receive your quotes. We do not receive your payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

Sharing

If you copy or share a message to a dealer, nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose where to send it.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't show ads or share your data with advertisers.
  • We don't contact dealers on your behalf or share your quotes with them.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Delete all my data to permanently delete your saved deals and settings from your phone. Deleting the app also removes them. The random ID in the iPhone Keychain stays, even after "Delete all my data" or reinstalling the app. To delete the records tied to it (the scan counts on our server and the purchase record at RevenueCat), email auracheck.app@gmail.com.

Children

SignSmart is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Security

Everything sent between the app, our server, Anthropic and RevenueCat is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Data on your phone is protected by your device's security.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to its processing. Most of your data stays on your device, where you can view and delete it in the app. For anything else, contact auracheck.app@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
